Questions about giving KinFM

Can it be a surprise?+

The card can. The recordings cannot: nothing is recorded or produced until your grandparent agrees in their own voice.

Can I give it from another country?+

Yes. What matters is the billing address of the person paying.

Audio Biography for US$49 as a one-time payment, instead of the US$99 list price. Taxes included. The same price in every country where it is available: with a billing address in the United Arab Emirates, the European Union, the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, or Argentina. If your country is not listed, email us at soporte@kinfm.com. Limited to 20 founding families.

Your grandparent can live in a different country.

What do they receive on Christmas Day?+

The card, printed or sent on WhatsApp, with your message. They can start recording whenever they’re ready.

What if they don’t want to?+

It’s their story, and they decide whether to tell it. The card is an invitation: your grandparent gives consent in their own voice at the start. If they prefer not to, we refund 100% to the original payment method, up to 12 months after payment.

Can I try it before paying?+

Yes: a free sample of about 1 minute in your grandparent’s real voice, verified by KinFM’s automated checks; one per family for the first 20 families, with no payment, card, or commitment, in the same countries as Founders. Request it by email: within one business day, we’ll email you from soporte@kinfm.com with a guide and a private link to upload their first recording, with consent in their own voice at the start; you’ll receive the sample within 7 days of our receiving the recording, and it does not include a chapter or a full episode. If you like it, you can continue with Founders for US$49 with the same guarantee; if you don’t continue, we’ll delete the recording and sample within 30 days, or sooner if you ask at privacidad@kinfm.com.

Is this therapy?+

No. KinFM is a family biography service, not therapy or medical care. It does not diagnose conditions or promise health benefits. If signs of crisis arise, the interview stops, local support resources are provided, and the session is flagged for human review. That material is never used in an episode.

What happens to the recordings and personal data?+

Recordings are processed with the paid Google Gemini API and are not used for model training. Stripe processes payments. Provider terms: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms. We do not share recordings with a printer: the printed book is not sold today. Data and recordings are deleted on request by emailing privacidad@kinfm.com. You can also request access, correction, or withdraw consent there. Keep your downloads: we cannot delete copies your family has downloaded or shared.

Who reviews the episodes?+

KinFM’s automated checks verify every episode: each quote must be word for word and linked to its exact place in the recording, every piece of historical context must have a source, and consent must be given in the storyteller’s own voice. Then the person who ordered it listens and approves it before sharing it. Questions about memories or dates are checked privately with the family.

Do grandchildren talk directly to the AI?+

No. An adult records or uploads the grandchildren’s questions so they can be woven into the conversation. The AI never speaks with children.
