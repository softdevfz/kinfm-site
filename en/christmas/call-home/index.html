    How the times are worked out

    • We check every minute of 24 and 25 December in your calendar. Your family’s local date is shown next to each time, because it can be a different day for them.
    • “Awake from” is included and “until” is not: 09:00 to 21:00 means up to 20:59. So if your day ends at 21:00 and theirs starts at that same moment, that doesn’t count as a shared time.
    • Hours can cross midnight, such as 22:00 to 06:00 for a night shift.
    • Time differences, including daylight saving, come from the time-zone data built into your browser. In December, for example, Sydney is on summer time and London is not.
    • If no minute works for everyone, you get the least-bad one: where whoever is furthest outside their usual hours is closest to them. Ties go to the smallest total time outside across everyone, then to the earliest minute.
