Privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Your family’s voices and memories are handled with care to create the episodes and book you request. This policy explains what data we collect, which service providers are involved, and how to request complete deletion.

Data and purpose

We process recordings, recorded voice consent, transcripts, memories people choose to share, recorded questions sent by an adult, and the contact and billing details needed to produce and deliver the digital biography and manage payment and support.

Consent

Before starting, the storyteller gives consent in their own voice; the paying relative cannot replace it. The producer identifies itself as AI, the producer of the family radio. We never clone voices. The AI does not talk to minors; an adult sends their recorded questions.

Providers

Recordings are processed with the paid Google Gemini API and are not used for model training. Stripe processes payments. Provider terms: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms. We do not share recordings with a printer: the printed book is not sold today.

If the interview takes place over WhatsApp, voice notes and messages go through WhatsApp, a Meta Platforms service, and its business platform, under their terms. We treat them like any other recording under this policy.

Deletion on request

Data and recordings are deleted on request by emailing privacidad@kinfm.com. You can also request access, correction, or withdraw consent there. Keep your downloads: we cannot delete copies your family has downloaded or shared.

Free sample: if you don’t continue with Founders, we’ll delete the recording and sample within 30 days, or sooner if you ask at privacidad@kinfm.com. If you buy Founders, the recording is used for your biography in line with the purposes described in this policy.

Personal agents

KinFM does not currently connect memories to agents or third-party services. If this is offered in the future, it will be disabled by default and will require the express consent of the person being interviewed, who may withdraw it at any time.

Sensitive material and crisis protocol

KinFM is not therapy or a medical service. If signs of suicide risk, self-harm, violence, or abuse arise during a conversation, the AI stops the interview, provides local support resources for the relevant country, and flags the session for human review. That material is permanently excluded from all episodes and chapters.

This website

This website loads Google Fonts, sending technical data such as your IP address to Google. It uses no tracking cookies. Cookie-free analytics is explained in “Analytics without cookies”. The language suggestion uses sessionStorage during the browser session. My story has no form: the link opens your email application.

If you arrive through a campaign link, we store only the channel code (for example, “li”) in sessionStorage for the session and add it to the payment link and the sample email subject, so we can see which channels work. That code does not identify you.

Analytics without cookies

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure site visits and performance using the browser’s Performance API, without storing data in the browser or accessing browser storage (such as cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, or IndexedDB). Cloudflare Web Analytics does not collect or use visitors’ personal data: although the service receives the IP address as part of normal HTTP request handling at the nearest Cloudflare data center (which may be in a different country or region), it discards the IP address there and does not store it in core databases or logs.
